# How to post a notice on the school website

Post notices by email, from the school Gmail account **ssvn.em.high.school@gmail.com**, on your phone or computer.

## Post a notice

1. Write a new email **to: ssvn.em.high.school+notice@gmail.com**
   (save it as a contact named *Website notice* so it's easy to find).
2. **Subject** = the notice title. Say what happens and when:
   *School closed on 20 October for Vijayadashami*
3. **Text** = the notice itself. Short paragraphs are best. Lines starting with `- ` become a list.
4. **Attach** PDFs or photos (JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB each) if you like. They appear as downloads.
5. Press **Send**.

Within about 5 minutes you get a reply in the same email thread: **"Published"**, with the link. The notice is on the website a minute or two after that.

## Choose a category (optional)

Put one of these in square brackets anywhere in the subject:

| Write | Shows as |
| --- | --- |
| `[Holiday]` | Holiday |
| `[Admissions]` | Admissions |
| `[Academics]` | Academics |
| `[Event]` | Event |
| `[Celebration]` | Celebration |
| *(nothing)* | General |

Add **`[Urgent]`** for closures and safety notices only. The notice is shown in red for 7 days.

Example subject: `[Holiday] [Urgent] School closed on 20 October for Vijayadashami`

## Add a Telugu title (optional)

Make the **first line** of the text:

`Telugu: విజయదశమి సందర్భంగా అక్టోబర్ 20న సెలవు`

Please have a teacher write the Telugu rather than using a translation app.

## Change or remove a notice

Reply in the **same email thread** (to the "Published" email or to your own):

- **To change it:** write the corrected text and send. To change the title, edit the subject too (keep the "Re:"). Attach files again only if you want to replace them.
- **To take it down:** reply with just the word **remove**.

You get a reply each time to confirm. You can change a notice this way for 30 days after first sending it; after that, ask the website maintainer.

## Add an event to the school calendar

Holidays, exams, events and meetings come from the school's **Google Calendar**, "Sri Sai Vidyanikethan E.M. High School Events". Signed in as the school account, in the Google Calendar app or at https://calendar.google.com:

1. Create the event **in the school calendar** (not your personal one).
2. **Holidays and exams:** tick **All day**; for several days, set the last day as the end date.
   **Meetings and events at a time:** set the start time. It shows on the website, e.g. "9:30 am".
3. Choose the type with the event's **colour**: **Banana** (yellow) = Holiday, **Sage** (green) = Exam, **Tangerine** (orange) = Event, **Graphite** (grey) = Meeting. Or write it in the title: `[Holiday] Dasara holidays`.
4. Optional: the **first line of the description** shows under the event, e.g. *school reopens 26 October*.
5. **Save.** The website updates in about 2 minutes. Changing or deleting the event updates the website the same way.

Parents can add the same calendar to their own phones from the **Add to your calendar** link on the Notices page.

## Prefer a browser?

You can also add, edit and delete notices at **https://app.pagescms.org**. Sign in with the school email address. Step-by-step: [pages-cms.md](pages-cms.md).

## Good to know

- Notices are **public**. Never include students' names, phone numbers or marks.
- Only emails **sent from the school account** are published. Emails from anyone else to the notice address are ignored.
- If something goes wrong, the reply explains what to fix. If no reply arrives within 10 minutes, tell the website maintainer.
