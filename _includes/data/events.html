{%- comment -%} Prepares the school calendar from _data/events.yml. Sets: cal_events — every event, oldest first (samples only when site.show_samples is true) upcoming_events — the next `limit` events that haven't ended yet (default 5) Dates in events.yml are quoted "YYYY-MM-DD" strings, so they sort and compare as text. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ev_today = site.time | date: "%Y-%m-%d" -%} {%- assign cal_events = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign ev_sorted = site.data.events | sort: "start" -%} {%- for ev in ev_sorted -%} {%- if ev.sample and site.show_samples != true -%}{%- continue -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign cal_events = cal_events | push: ev -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign ev_limit = include.limit | default: 5 -%} {%- assign upcoming_events = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for ev in cal_events -%} {%- assign ev_end = ev.end | default: ev.start -%} {%- if ev_end >= ev_today and upcoming_events.size < ev_limit -%} {%- assign upcoming_events = upcoming_events | push: ev -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
